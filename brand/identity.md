# Neuro Forge Identity

Explore. Understand. Connect. Invent.

## Personal Motto

Ne jugez pas le style. Jugez les résultats.

FORGED FOR ATYPICAL THINKERS.

## Visual Direction

- pixel art monochrome;
- black and white;
- alchemical red accent;
- no classic gradient;
- controlled pixel dissolution allowed;
- no hammer;
- no decorative anvil;
- no artificial N or F integration;
- circular glyph;
- simplified brain;
- balance between organic and digital;
- no decoration without function;
- readable at 32 x 32 pixels;
- symbol works without text.
