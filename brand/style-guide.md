# Style Guide

## Palette

- Black: `#050505`
- White: `#f7f3ea`
- Alchemical red: `#b11226`

## Rules

- Use black pages with white structure and red accents.
- Keep contrast high.
- Avoid decorative gradients.
- Use pixel cuts, hard lines and circular glyphs.
- Preserve logo readability down to 32 x 32 px.
