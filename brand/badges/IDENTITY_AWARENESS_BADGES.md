## Usage rules

- Identity/awareness badges are opt-in and secondary to project/status badges.
- Do not infer or assign a health/disability badge to a contributor or project automatically.
- `Swiss Made` is an origin claim and must only be used where that claim is actually justified; otherwise prefer `Made in the Alps` or omit origin badges.
- Keep awareness badges in an `Identity / Awareness` section rather than presenting them as build, quality, safety or certification indicators.
