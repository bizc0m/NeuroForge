# Taillandier
**Taillandier** is a uniquely French word with no exact equivalent in English.
A taillandier is not simply a blacksmith. A taillandier is a craftsman who forges purpose-built edged tools.
Each tool is designed for one specific task.
The value of the tool lies in its precision, its edge and its ability to perform the work for which it was created.
Neuro Forge applies the same philosophy to software.
It creates dedicated cognitive tools designed to cut through complexity with precision.
