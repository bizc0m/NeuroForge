# Principles
1. Dedicated tools over universal platforms.
2. Precision over feature count.
3. Reliability over novelty.
4. Understandable systems over opaque complexity.
5. Useful results over impressive demonstrations.
6. Every feature must justify its existence.
7. Every release must be tested.
8. AI-assisted development does not remove human responsibility.
9. Software must save time, not consume it.
